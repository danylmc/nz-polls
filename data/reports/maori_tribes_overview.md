# NZES2023 Māori Voter Tribes - Overview

Analysis of 695 Māori voters from the 2023 Election Study, clustered into 2 tribes based on political attitudes.

## Methodology Note

This analysis uses the same 40 clustering variables as the main analysis, but applied only to respondents who identify as Māori (`mmaori == 1`). Weighting uses `mwgt` (Māori-specific weight) for population inference.

## Tribe Profiles

### Māori Tribe 1: Moderate Māori

**Size**: 278 respondents (40.0% of Māori sample)

**Political orientation**: centrist (L-R mean: 6.0)

**Party vote**: Non-vote (32%), National (26%), Labour (14%)

**Demographics**: Average age 47, 59% male

**Key attitudes**:
- + C9b (z=0.81)
- + C12i (z=0.81)
- + C9c (z=0.81)

### Māori Tribe 2: Progressive Māori

**Size**: 417 respondents (60.0% of Māori sample)

**Political orientation**: left-leaning (L-R mean: 3.9)

**Party vote**: Labour (37%), Non-vote (19%), Te Pāti Māori (14%)

**Demographics**: Average age 45, 42% male

**Key attitudes**:
